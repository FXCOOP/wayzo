🚀 Implementation Roadmap & API Endpoints

POST /api/user/plan/:id/assistant/analyze

AI analyzes trip 5 days before departure, checks weather, prices, availability

POST /api/user/plan/:id/notifications/schedule

Schedule automated notifications (check-in reminders, activity alerts, budget warnings)

GET /api/user/plan/:id/bookings

Extract and track all bookings from itinerary (flights, hotels, activities)

POST /api/user/plan/:id/checklist/generate

Auto-generate packing checklist based on destination, weather, activities, duration

POST /api/user/plan/:id/documents

Upload and encrypt travel documents (passport, visa, insurance)

GET /api/user/plan/:id/weather/forecast

Real-time weather updates and packing recommendations

POST /api/user/plan/:id/share

Generate shareable link with permissions (view-only, edit, collaborate)

POST /api/user/plan/:id/budget/track

Log expenses, categorize spending, get budget alerts

💡 More Feature Ideas

🎭 Activity Recommendations Engine High Priority

AI suggests activities based on user preferences, time of day, weather, and proximity. "You have 2 free hours near Museum Island - want to visit Pergamon Museum?"

🗺️ Offline Maps & Guides High Priority

Download offline maps, itinerary, and key info for areas with poor connectivity. Essential for international travel.

💬 AI Travel Chatbot High Priority

"Where should I eat dinner near my hotel?" - Get instant answers about your trip, recommendations, and help rescheduling.

🎁 Loyalty Program Integration Medium Priority

Connect airline miles, hotel points, credit card rewards. Track points earned and suggest best redemption options.

👥 Group Trip Coordinator Medium Priority

Poll for dates, split costs, vote on activities, shared packing lists. Perfect for family trips or friend groups.

📊 Trip Analytics Dashboard Low Priority

Visualize your travel stats: countries visited, total miles flown, money spent, carbon footprint, trip memories over time.

🎯 Smart Re-Optimizer High Priority

If a museum is closed or weather is bad, AI instantly suggests alternatives and rearranges your day for optimal experience.

🔄 Trip Templates & Cloning Medium Priority

Loved your Berlin trip? Clone it for Prague or share it as a template for other users to customize.

🏆 Gamification & Achievements Low Priority

Earn badges for visiting landmarks, trying local foods, completing challenges. Make travel more engaging and shareable.

🌍 Carbon Footprint Tracker Medium Priority

Calculate trip emissions, suggest eco-friendly alternatives, offset options. Appeal to conscious travelers.

📞 Local SIM/eSIM Recommendations Medium Priority

Suggest best data plans for your destination, provide eSIM activation guides, track data usage.

🎵 Destination Playlist Generator Low Priority

AI curates Spotify playlists with local music, popular artists, and mood-based tracks for each destination.
