Scoring Algorithm

Multi-dimensional scoring combining company fit, buyer intent, and activity momentum. Transparent and explainable.

Component Signal Points Category
Company Size Enterprise (500+ employees) +20 Fit (0-50)
Mid-Market (100-499 employees) +15 Fit
SMB (20-99 employees) +10 Fit
ABM Status On ABM target list +15 Fit
Industry Match Tech / MSP / Cybersecurity / Fintech / SaaS +10 Fit
Job Title Decision-maker (CTO, CIO, VP, Director) +5 Fit
Influencer (Manager, Lead, Senior) +3 Fit
Form Type Demo Request +35 Intent (0-40)
Pricing Inquiry +30 Intent
Trial Signup +28 Intent
Contact Form +15 Intent
Recency Active today +10 Momentum (0-10)
Active within 7 days +7 Momentum
Free Email gmail / yahoo / hotmail / outlook -15 Penalty
Missing Phone No phone or < 7 chars -5 Penalty
# ALS2 Scoring Formula
def calculate_score(contact):
    fit     = company_size + abm_status + industry + job_title   # 0-50
    intent  = form_type_signal                                 # 0-40
    momentum = recency_score                              # 0-10
    penalties = free_email + missing_phone                # -20 max

    total = max(0, min(100, fit + intent + momentum + penalties))
    return { "total": total, "tier": determine_tier(total) }

Claude Desktop Integration

10 MCP Tools

Claude Desktop connects to HubSpot through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), enabling natural-language CRM operations.

1
score_lead
Score a contact using the ALS2 engine. Returns fit, intent, momentum, routing (AE/SDR/Nurture) and priority (P0/P1/P2).
2
get_contact
Retrieve full contact details from HubSpot including all ALS2 scoring properties.
3
sync_to_salesforce
Sync a scored lead to Salesforce as an Opportunity with AI-generated insights.
4
generate_one_pager
Generate a professional one-pager summary using GPT-5 Nano. Templates: executive, technical, partnership.
5
get_hot_leads
Retrieve all P0 (hot) leads requiring immediate attention from HubSpot.
6
create_task
Create a follow-up task in HubSpot associated with a specific contact.
7
setup_hubspot_properties
Auto-create all 13 ALS2 custom properties + 4 dynamic tier lists in HubSpot. One-time setup.
8
set_lead_tier
Calculate and write the lead tier (1-4), SLA, routing, and priority for a contact.
9
create_hubspot_property
Create a single custom property on contacts or companies in HubSpot.
10
mark_target_accounts
Scan all scored contacts, find their companies, mark Tier 1/2 companies as HubSpot Target Accounts.

What's Next

ALS2 Roadmap

Planned enhancements for the next phase of development.

Phase 1: Smart Routing Engine

Round-robin assignment with capacity awareness. Auto-assign leads to available AEs/SDRs based on workload, territory, and expertise match.

Phase 2: Slack Real-Time Alerts

Instant Slack notifications per tier. P0 Hot leads trigger #sales-alerts immediately. Daily digest of warm leads. Weekly cold lead nurture report.

Phase 3: Score Decay & Re-Scoring

Automatic score decay over time. Re-score contacts on new activity. Historical score tracking with trend analysis and score change alerts.

Phase 4: ML-Based Scoring

Train models on closed-won deals to identify patterns. Replace rule-based scoring with ML predictions. Continuous model improvement with feedback loops.

Phase 5: Full Revenue Intelligence

End-to-end pipeline visibility from first touch to closed-won. AI-generated deal insights, risk alerts, and coaching recommendations for sales managers.
